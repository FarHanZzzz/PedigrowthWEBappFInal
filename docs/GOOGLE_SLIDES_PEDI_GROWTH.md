Title: Pedi-Growth: the living room, connected to the clinic.

- Today, many Bangladeshi children with cerebral palsy are identified around age 5.

SLIDE 12 — Close (sparse)

Line: Thank you.
Name: Farhan Sadeque

## 20-second backup answers

**Does it diagnose CP?**  
No. It structures what a walking clip showed so a clinician can decide the next step.

**Why Bangladesh?**  
Large child population, late typical identification of cerebral palsy (~5 years), and most of those children never received rehab.

**What if the video is bad?**  
We say the clip cannot be trusted and ask for a retake.

**Who is it for?**  
Parents who can record a walking child, and clinicians who need a packet — not a WhatsApp guess.
